06 — COMMON QUESTIONS

Things you may wonder.

Is the K50 membership fee paid every month?

No. It covers two months. You pay it in your first month, not your second, and renew it in the third month if you continue.

Do individual services require membership?

No. The individual services are one-time purchases and do not require the K50 membership fee.

Can I request personal skills coaching at my residence?

Yes, subject to agreement. Any additional travel fee depends on the distance and will be discussed with you before the session is confirmed.

Do the plans include diet guidance?

No. THENIX coaching focuses on bodyweight training, exercise guidance, technique and skill development. Diet guidance is not included.

Do I need to be an advanced athlete?

No. The suitable starting point depends on your current ability and the service you choose.

Are individual training plans the same as ongoing coaching?

No. A plan is a one-time written programme. Ongoing coaching involves interaction, feedback and guidance over time.
